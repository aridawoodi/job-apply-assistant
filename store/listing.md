# Chrome Web Store listing: Job Apply Assistant

## Name
Job Apply Assistant

## Summary (short description, max 132 characters; same as manifest "description")
Fills job applications and tailors your resume with a free local AI. Private: your data stays on your computer. Never clicks Submit.

## Category
Productivity (alternative: Tools)

## Language
English

## Detailed description
Apply to jobs faster without giving your data to anyone.

Job Apply Assistant fills job applications on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby (and basic Workday and LinkedIn Easy Apply) from your profile, and uses a free AI that runs on your own computer (Ollama) for everything else. Nothing is uploaded: no account, no subscription, no cloud.

WHAT IT DOES
• Fills the application for you: name, contact details, links, work authorization, education, your resume file and more. Every field is colour-coded so you can see what came from your profile, what the AI drafted, and what still needs you.
• Chats with you about the rest, one question at a time, with tap-to-answer buttons. It drafts the "Why do you want to work here?" answers in your own voice.
• Tailors your resume to each job: type "tailor my resume for this job". It suggests rewording and reordering using only what's already in your resume. Invented employers, titles, dates, numbers or tools are blocked automatically, and you approve every change. Download Word, PDF or both, named "First Last - Company - Job Title", and attach it to the application with one click.
• Your rules for tailoring, like "Keep to 2 pages", "Use US spelling" or "Don't change job titles".
• Tracks your applications: when you press Submit, the tracker updates to Submitted, then Applied when the site confirms. Export to CSV.
• Never presses Submit, Next or Apply. You review and submit yourself.

EASY SETUP, NO TERMINAL
1. Install the extension. A setup page opens.
2. It finds Ollama (or gives you a one-click download for Mac or Windows) and downloads the right AI model for your computer, with a progress bar.
3. Drop in your resume (PDF or Word). Your profile fills itself in; you check it and answer a few quick questions. Then try it on the built-in practice application.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your profile, resume, answers and tracker are stored only in your browser.
• The AI runs on your computer. The extension makes no other network requests: no analytics, no tracking, no remote code.
• Equal-opportunity (EEO) answers, salary and personal details are never sent to the AI. EEO questions are optional and you can always decline.

REQUIREMENTS
• Chrome 110 or newer on Mac, Windows or Linux.
• Ollama (free, from ollama.com). 8 GB of memory is enough; about 3 GB of disk space for the small model.

## Single purpose (Privacy practices tab)
Job Apply Assistant helps the user complete job applications: it fills application forms from the user's own profile and tailors the user's resume to the job, using an AI model that runs locally on the user's computer.

## Remote code (Privacy practices tab)
No, I am not using remote code. All JavaScript is packaged in the extension (including the bundled pdf.js, JSZip and jsPDF libraries). No code is downloaded or evaluated at runtime.

## Data usage (Privacy practices tab): what to tick
Recommended (honest, conservative) answers. All of this data stays on the user's computer:
- **Personally identifiable information: yes** (name, email, phone, address and work history the user enters or imports from their resume).
- **Website content: yes** (the text of the job application page, its form questions and the job description, read to fill the form and tailor the resume).
- **Health information: yes, to be safe** (only the optional, self-identified disability status a user may give for equal-opportunity questions; it's never sent to the AI).
- Financial and payment information, Authentication information, Personal communications, Location (GPS), Web history, User activity: **no**.

Certify all three:
- I do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, outside of the approved use cases.
- I do not use or transfer user data for purposes that are unrelated to my item's single purpose.
- I do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Note for the "data is transmitted" questions: data is processed on the user's device and sent only to the Ollama server on the user's own computer (127.0.0.1); it is never sent to the developer.
