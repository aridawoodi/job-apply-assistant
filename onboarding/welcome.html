How to use it

  1. Open a job application on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday or LinkedIn Easy Apply.
  2. Click the purple ⚡ Fill application button (bottom right), or the toolbar icon.
  3. Answer what's left in the chat, review, and press the site's Submit button yourself. The assistant never presses it.
