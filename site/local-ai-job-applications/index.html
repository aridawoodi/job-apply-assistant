Job applications with local AI: Ollama or Chrome’s built-in AI

Your resume stays on your computer, and the AI writing costs nothing to run. Here’s how it works and what you need.

Add to Chrome, it’s freeSee how it works

Updated October 31, 2026 · by Ari Dawoodi, the developer of AI Job Application Assistant

Job applications are full of personal data. Running the AI on your own computer means your resume isn’t sent to a cloud model, and nobody pays per request, which is why every AI feature in AI Job Application Assistant is free.

Two local engines, picked for you

Chrome’s built-in AI

Chrome 138 or newer can run a small built-in model on your device. There’s nothing to install. It usually needs about 16 GB of memory or a strong graphics card, and Chrome decides whether your device qualifies.

Ollama

The free Ollama app runs open models on Mac, Windows and Linux. 8 GB of memory is enough for the small model (about 3 GB of disk). With 16 GB or more, the setup suggests a larger model that writes better. If both engines are available, the extension falls back automatically.

What the AI is asked to do

  • Draft open answers like “Why do you want to work here?” from your resume, in your voice.
  • Tailor your resume by rewording and reordering what’s already there. Invented employers, titles, dates, numbers and tools are blocked.
  • Write cover letters with the same fact checks.
  • Never answer equal-opportunity questions or see your salary. Those are never sent to the AI.

No terminal needed for Ollama

Ollama normally rejects requests from browser extensions unless you set OLLAMA_ORIGINS in a terminal. Instead, the extension adds a narrow declarativeNetRequest rule that removes the Origin header only on requests the extension itself makes to your local Ollama address. Web pages are not affected. The details are in the README.

Common fixes

  • “Waiting for Ollama…”: install Ollama with the button on the setup page and open it.
  • Ollama refused the connection: click “Try the automatic fix”. If that fails, follow the manual steps shown below it.
  • Slow first answer: the first request loads the model, which can take up to a minute. Close heavy tabs and use the recommended model.

Questions

Which model does it use with Ollama?

It suggests qwen2.5:3b for 8 GB computers and qwen2.5:7b when you have 16 GB or more. The setup page downloads it for you with a progress bar.

Can websites talk to my Ollama through the extension?

No. The extension removes the Origin header only on its own requests to your local Ollama address. Web pages are unaffected, so Ollama still blocks them.

Does it work offline?

The AI runs locally, so drafting works without a cloud service. You still need the internet to load job pages.
