Free job application autofill: what’s actually free

Almost every tool says “free”. Here’s what that covers in each one, and how to get the AI writing free too.

Short answer: form autofill is free in most tools. AI writing (answers to open questions, tailored resumes and cover letters) is where you meet credits, allowances and subscriptions. If you want the AI writing free as well, use a tool whose AI runs on your own computer.

Three different things are called “free”

1. Autofill from your profile

Your name, contact details, work history and education are copied into the form. This is cheap to provide, so almost every tool offers it free, sometimes “unlimited” and sometimes limited to certain sites or one application at a time.

2. AI writing

“Why do you want to work here?” answers, tailored resumes and cover letters. Cloud tools pay for every AI request, so this is usually a trial, a credit balance or a subscription.

3. Auto-apply

The tool submits applications for you. That’s a different product with different risks: you don’t see each application before it goes. Free plans usually allow only a handful.

How to choose

  1. Count how many applications you send. A few a week? Most free autofill plans are enough. Dozens a day with AI answers? That’s where limits bite.
  2. Decide where your resume may go. Cloud tools keep your profile in an account and send text to AI providers. Local tools keep it on your computer.
  3. Check the sites you apply on. Coverage varies a lot. Try each tool on a real application before you commit.
  4. Keep the Submit button. Tools that fill and stop let you catch mistakes before an employer sees them.
  5. Check your computer if you want local AI. 8 GB of memory runs a small model; 16 GB runs a better one.

How AI Job Application Assistant stays free

I built it as a solo developer, and it has no servers. The AI runs on your computer, either Chrome’s built-in AI or the free Ollama app, so there is no per-use cost for me to pass on. It’s MIT open source. Every feature, including AI answers, tailoring and cover letters, is free with no limits.

Questions

What’s the difference between autofill and auto-apply?

Autofill fills the form and stops, so you review and press Submit. Auto-apply submits applications for you, often in the cloud and without a review step. This extension only autofills.

Why do most tools charge for AI but not autofill?

Autofill copies your saved details into fields, which costs the company almost nothing. AI writing calls a cloud language model that they pay for each time. A tool that runs the AI on your computer doesn’t have that cost.

Is free autofill safe?

Read the privacy policy before you upload a resume anywhere. Check where your profile is stored, whether an account is needed and which AI providers see your data.
