A free, private alternative to Simplify+ AI features

Keep free autofill, and get AI answers, tailored resumes and cover letters without a subscription or an account.

Add to Chrome, it’s freeSee how it works

Updated October 31, 2026 · by Ari Dawoodi, the developer of AI Job Application Assistant

Simplify Copilot is a popular, well-made autofill extension, and its basic autofill is free. Its AI features (tailored resumes, cover letters and AI answers) are part of the paid Simplify+ plan. If those AI features are what you want, and you’d rather not create an account or send your resume to a cloud service, this page is for you.

Side by side

When Simplify is the better choice

  • You apply on many different application systems and want the widest coverage.
  • You want a job board, recruiter features and an iPhone app in the same account.
  • Your computer is older or has little memory, so local AI would be slow.

When AI Job Application Assistant is the better choice

  • You want AI answers, tailored resumes and cover letters without paying or counting credits.
  • You don’t want an account, and you want your resume to stay on your computer.
  • You apply mostly through Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby forms, which are common at tech companies and startups.
  • You want to read the code of the tool that handles your personal data.

Switching takes about a minute

  1. Add the extension to Chrome. A welcome page opens.
  2. Import your resume (PDF or Word) and check what it found.
  3. Try it on the built-in practice application, then on a real one.
  4. Set up the local AI when you want help with open questions, tailoring and cover letters. The setup page guides you with no terminal.

Questions

Can I use both?

Yes, but two autofill extensions on the same form can fight over the same fields. Pause one of them on sites where you use the other.

Do I need to export anything from Simplify?

No. Import your resume (PDF or Word) and the extension builds your profile from it. You check it once.

Is Simplify Copilot free?

Its autofill is free. AI resume tailoring, cover letters and AI answers need Simplify+, according to Simplify’s help center (checked October 31, 2026).
