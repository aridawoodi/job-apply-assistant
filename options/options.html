Profile

Stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local). Blank fields are left for you to fill on each form.

Resume

Your master resume: attached to resume/CV upload fields, used to fill your profile, and the starting point for tailored versions. PDF or Word (.docx). Word keeps your exact layout when tailoring.

No resume saved.

Tailoring

Type "tailor my resume for this job" in the assistant chat on any job page. Your local AI suggests rewording and reordering using only facts already in your resume; you approve each change.

My tailoring rules

Given to the AI every time. Rules marked checked are also verified automatically; AI rules are followed by the model and worth a quick look.

    Saved resumes

    Tailored resumes you created (newest first, last 60 kept). Linked to the matching job in your tracker.

    Writing style

    Used only for long answers ("Why us?", "Tell us about…") so drafts sound like you. Paste a few paragraphs you wrote yourself (cover letters, emails, posts) and describe your tone. The local model copies the voice, not the content. The drafts also use your resume text and the job description from the page.

    Answer bank

    Reusable answers. Questions on a form are matched to these by similar wording before anything is sent to the local AI (green, "from your saved answers"). Answers you type on job sites are learned here automatically when "Remember my answers" is on. {company} in a company-specific entry is replaced with the company you're applying to.

    Fields to fill

    Untick anything you'd rather fill yourself. Turned-off fields are left empty and listed as "turned off" in the panel. Consent boxes are never ticked by the assistant, whatever you choose here.

    Local AI

    Auto tries Chrome's built-in AI first (if available on your device), then falls back to Ollama. Chrome built-in AI (Gemini Nano) requires Chrome 138+ and about 16 GB RAM or a strong GPU. On 8 GB machines use Ollama.

    Ollama settings

    With 8 GB of memory, qwen2.5:3b is the fastest reliable choice; with 16 GB or more, qwen2.5:7b writes better. Reasoning models (qwen3.x, deepseek-r1) are asked not to "think", but are still slower. Need help connecting or downloading a model? Run setup again.

    General

    Backups

    Your profile, answer bank, settings and tracker are snapshotted automatically (on updates, browser start, and at most every 30 minutes while they change). The last 5 snapshots are kept inside the extension. For safety outside the browser, save a backup file now and then. Never click "Remove" on the extension in chrome://extensions: that deletes all of its data, including these snapshots.

    SnapshotReasonVersionContents

    Import / export

    Replaces the profile and answer bank with the file's contents (resume and settings too, if present).

    Application tracker

    Logged when you fill an application and when you submit one yourself. Submitted = you pressed Submit, waiting for the site's confirmation · Applied = confirmation seen · Error - not sent = the form showed an error after Submit. Change the status by hand any time (Interview, Offer, … are never overwritten automatically).
