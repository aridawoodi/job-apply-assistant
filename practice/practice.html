Data Analyst

Remote · Full-time · Practice posting

About the role

Example Co is looking for a Data Analyst to turn product and operations data into clear decisions. You will partner with product managers and engineers, build dashboards, and investigate questions end to end.

What you'll do

  • Write SQL to answer product and business questions, and automate recurring reports.
  • Build and maintain dashboards that teams use every day.
  • Design and analyze experiments, and explain results to non-technical partners.
  • Improve data quality by working with engineering on tracking and pipelines.

What we're looking for

  • 2+ years of experience in analytics, data engineering or a similar role.
  • Strong SQL and experience with Python or R.
  • Clear written communication and curiosity about how the business works.
  • Experience with a BI tool (Looker, Tableau or similar) is a plus.
